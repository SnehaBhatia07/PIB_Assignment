### 1. What is RAG, and when would you use it?

RAG stands for Retrieval-Augmented Generation. It allows an AI model to first retrieve relevant information from external sources and then use that information to generate an answer.

For example, a company can use RAG to search its internal HR documents when an employee asks about a company policy.

I would use RAG when the information is private, frequently updated, or stored in external documents such as company policies, manuals, or knowledge bases.

Simple flow:
Documents → Retrieve relevant information → LLM → Answer

### 2. What is the difference between AI, Machine Learning, and Generative AI?

AI - It is the broader field of creating systems that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

Machine Learning - It is a part of AI where systems learn patterns from data instead of being programmed with every rule manually.

Generative AI - It focuses on creating new content such as text, images, code, audio, or video.

In simple terms:
AI = broad field
Machine Learning = learns from data
Generative AI = creates new content

### 3. What is a prompt in AI?

A prompt is the instruction or input given to an AI model to tell it what we want.

For example:
"Explain React in simple language with one example."

A good prompt usually gives a clear task, relevant context, and any important requirements.

Therefore, a prompt is how we communicate our requirement to an AI model.
